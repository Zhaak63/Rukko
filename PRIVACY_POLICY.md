## 2. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features enabled in the version you use, Rukko may process:
- **Discord identifiers and profile information** made available through Discord's APIs, such as a Discord user ID, username, or avatar.
- **Gameplay data**, such as scores, match results, statistics, progression, and game settings.
- **Technical and diagnostic data**, such as error reports, timestamps, and information needed to investigate bugs or protect the service.
- **Information you send voluntarily**, such as bug reports, suggestions, or messages to the support team.

Only data actually required for Rukko's features should be collected. Rukko should not request Discord passwords or user tokens.

## 3. Why information may be used

Information may be used, where applicable, to:
- Operate the game and provide its features.
- Manage playtests and player access.
- Save gameplay progress and statistics.
- Detect cheating, abuse, security incidents, and technical problems.
- Respond to support requests and improve the game.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Legal basis

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies, the legal basis depends on the specific processing and may include providing the service, legitimate interests such as security and debugging, consent where required, or compliance with a legal obligation. The actual legal basis should be confirmed for each data use.

## 5. Discord and other service providers

Rukko may rely on Discord to provide Discord-related features. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own terms and privacy policy.

Rukko may use hosting, database, or error-reporting providers if these services are actually configured. Any provider that receives personal data should be identified in the final policy where required.

Rukko does not sell personal information. Do not add this statement unless it accurately reflects the project's practices.

## 6. Retention

Information should be kept only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law. Retention periods should be specified for each data category once the game's storage and deletion behavior is known.

During playtesting, gameplay data may be reset. A reset of game progress does not necessarily mean that all technical logs or support messages have been deleted.

## 7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect stored information. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of your personal data, to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, contact the Rukko project team through its official support channels. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In France, this is the CNIL.

## 9. Children
